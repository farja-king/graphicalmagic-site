The Toolkit

Four tools. One workflow.

Every step from "customer sent me a blurry JPEG" to "file's on the press" — without switching apps.

Print Prep

Clean backgrounds without touching a lasso tool

Drop in a photo or logo and let smart edge detection or the local AI model pull the subject out cleanly — including tricky enclosed gaps like the inside of letters.

  • Smart edge, colour-key or AI extraction modes
  • Hole removal for trapped background pixels
  • Halftone, colour reduction & garment preview built in
Print Prep
Vector Prep

Photo to crisp vector, without the guesswork

Trace raster artwork into clean, editable paths, then fine-tune detail and touch up stray pixels — all inside a guided, step-by-step panel.

  • Upscale → Crop → Trace → Detail → Touch-up → Export
  • Live source/vector comparison as you tune
  • Export straight to clean, scalable SVG
Vector Prep
DTF Gang Sheets

Nest designs and know your cost before you print

Autofill your sheet, drag to arrange, and see film, ink and powder cost estimates update live — so nothing goes to press until the numbers make sense.

  • Auto-arrange for minimum sheet length
  • Live cost-to-produce breakdown
  • Text, colour and sheet-size controls in one place

Upscale

Rescue low-res artwork with local AI

Blow up a pixelated logo up to 8x without leaving your desktop. Pick photo or illustration mode, crop first to save time, and compare before/after with a live slider.

  • 2x, 4x, 6x and 8x scale factors
  • Photo/realistic vs. illustration/line-art modes
  • Runs fully offline — nothing leaves your PC
